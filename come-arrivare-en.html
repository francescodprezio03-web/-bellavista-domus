Practical information · Torre a Mare

Getting here

Torre a Mare is a district of Bari on the Adriatic coast, south of the city. It is easy to reach by car, and by public transport a single bus from the central station brings you here.

By car

Take the Torre a Mare centro exit: from there to the house is about two minutes. It is the simplest way to arrive, and since parking is inside the property you will not need to look for a space.

From Bari airport

Karol Wojtyła airport is about 20 minutes away by car. At peak times, with traffic around Bari, allow 25.

By public transport it takes two steps: first the connection from the airport to Bari central station, then the bus to Torre a Mare described below.

By public transport

Bus 12 (or 12/) leaves from Bari central station and runs to Torre a Mare. This is the connection we recommend: it is direct and drops you close to the house.

Torre a Mare also has its own railway stop on the Bari–Lecce line, but it is about a 15-minute walk from the station to the house: with luggage, the bus is more comfortable.

Parking

Two free private parking spaces inside the property, with room for scooters and bicycles too. Access is easy for cars up to 4.60 metres; longer vehicles need a little more manoeuvring, but they do fit. Outside the property, on-street parking is free and unrestricted.

Arrival and keys

Check-in is in person, and we agree the time beforehand on WhatsApp, so neither of us is left waiting. Arrival is from 3:00 pm and departure by 11:00 am. If your flight or train times are awkward, do write to us: whenever the calendar allows, we try to accommodate you.

Once you are here

The sea is ten metres away: you cross the seafront road — a quiet through road — and you are on the sandy cove in front of the house.

For shopping and errands a car is handy: the best-stocked supermarket, the pharmacy, the cash machine and the tobacconist are all about five minutes away by car. For the beach, the harbour and the village, though, you will not need it — everything is walkable.
