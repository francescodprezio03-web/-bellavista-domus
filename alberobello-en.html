What to See in Alberobello

Alberobello, a UNESCO World Heritage site, is the most iconic destination inland from the coast, about 50 minutes from Torre a Mare.

The Trulli and Rione Monti

Over 1,000 trulli — the distinctive dry-stone buildings with conical roofs — cluster in the Rione Monti district: a sea of grey cone roofs that makes Alberobello unlike anywhere else in the world. Many trulli today house local craft shops, selling ceramics, regional products and souvenirs.

Rione Aia Piccola

Less touristy than Rione Monti, this district keeps the authentic atmosphere of trulli that are still lived in, with quiet lanes and striking views: the right place to experience Alberobello away from the crowds, in a more intimate, residential setting.

Trullo Sovrano

The town's only two-storey trullo, now turned into a museum, tells the story and building techniques behind these one-of-a-kind dwellings, from the dry-stone construction to the period furniture preserved inside.

If Alberobello feels too touristy

Alberobello is beautiful but very busy. In the same area, the Valle d'Itria, there are towns just as lovely and far quieter: Locorotondo, Cisternino, Ostuni and Martina Franca. If you only have one day, consider pairing one of them with Alberobello rather than stopping there alone: it is a short drive and a completely different mood.

Where to park

Designated car parks are easy to find. As in Monopoli, after 7 pm is the most convenient time — and the nicest for walking among the trulli.

Getting There

About 50 minutes by car from Torre a Mare; a car is recommended, as the inland location is less well served by train.
