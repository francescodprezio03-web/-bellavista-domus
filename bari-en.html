What to See in Bari

Bari, the regional capital, is just 15 minutes from Torre a Mare and the ideal gateway for exploring the whole of Puglia.

Bari Vecchia

The old town is a maze of alleys where women still hand-roll orecchiette pasta on tables set up outside their front doors, amid hidden little squares, ancient churches and washing strung between balconies. It's one of the city's most authentic experiences, and the best way to discover it is simply to get lost among its streets.

Basilica of San Nicola

Home to the relics of Saint Nicholas of Bari — the historical figure behind Santa Claus — the basilica is a pilgrimage site for Catholics and Orthodox Christians from around the world. This Romanesque church, with its imposing white façade, is one of the city's most important religious and architectural landmarks.

The Seafront Promenade

One of the longest seafront promenades in Europe, perfect for an evening stroll with views over the Adriatic: a stretch of Liberty-style buildings, gardens and sea views that accompanies the city into sunset.

Petruzzelli Theatre

One of Italy's most important opera houses, and a symbol of the city's cultural rebirth after the 1991 fire that nearly destroyed it completely. Today it hosts operas, concerts and prestigious lyrical seasons.

Getting There

15 minutes by car, by train, or even by bicycle along the coast from Torre a Mare.
