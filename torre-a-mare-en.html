What to See in Torre a Mare

Just steps from Bellavista Domus, Torre a Mare is the fishing village that gives its name to the whole area: a handful of streets gathered around a small fishermen's harbour, with the Adriatic in view from almost every corner.

The Origins: The 16th-Century Watchtower

The name comes from the ancient watchtower that still overlooks the harbour today. Like many of the coastal towers dotted along the Apulian shoreline, it was built during the 16th century, under Spanish rule, as part of a defensive network designed to spot Saracen pirate raids and warn the inland villages. Today the tower has become the quiet symbol of what was once a defensive outpost, and remains the first landmark for anyone arriving in the village.

The Harbour and the Promenade

The small fishing and tourist harbour is the heart of Torre a Mare: colourful boats, nets left out to dry and, in the evening, the lights of the venues overlooking the water. The promenade that runs alongside it is perfect for a sunset walk, when the sky over the Adriatic turns pink and orange behind the silhouette of the tower.

Sea and Coves

The coastline here alternates low cliffs and small inlets with stretches of free public beach, ideal for those looking for a quieter swim away from the busier beach clubs. The water is clear and the low coastline makes the area suitable for families with children, as well as for anyone who enjoys snorkelling among the rocks.

Where to Eat

Around the harbour you'll find trattorias and seafood restaurants serving traditional Bari-style cuisine: raw fish, orecchiette with mussels, mixed fried seafood. It's the perfect area for a fresh seafood dinner with a view of the water, just a few minutes' walk from Bellavista Domus.

Nearby

Torre a Mare is an excellent starting point for exploring the rest of the Bari coastline: the centre of Bari, with its promenade and old town, is about ten minutes away by car or just a few minutes by train (the village has its own stop on the Bari–Lecce line); heading south along the coast you'll soon reach the cliffs of Polignano a Mare and the old town of Monopoli, while inland lie the famous trulli of Alberobello, a UNESCO World Heritage site.

The Sea of Torre a Mare, Right at Home

Bellavista Domus is just 10 metres from the beach, in the heart of this area.
